# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in the `fivem-doctor` project a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone.

We welcome contributions from people with different backgrounds, experience levels, and perspectives.

## Expected Behavior

Examples of behavior that contribute to a positive environment include:

- Being respectful and considerate of others.
- Giving constructive feedback.
- Accepting differing opinions and technical approaches.
- Focusing criticism on ideas, code, and behavior rather than individuals.
- Helping create a collaborative and productive environment.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Trolling or deliberate disruption of discussions.
- Threats or intimidation.
- Publishing private or personal information without permission.
- Sexual harassment or other unwelcome sexual behavior.
- Other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate or harmful in a professional open-source community.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainer through the project's GitHub repository.

Please provide enough information to understand what happened, including relevant links or screenshots when appropriate.

Reports will be reviewed by the project maintainer and handled as reasonably and fairly as possible.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to project spaces, including GitHub Issues, Pull Requests, discussions, and other project-related interactions.

## Enforcement Guidelines

The project maintainer may take appropriate action in response to unacceptable behavior, including:

1. Asking a participant to stop the behavior.
2. Removing or editing inappropriate content.
3. Temporarily restricting participation.
4. Permanently restricting participation in project spaces for serious or repeated violations.

The response will depend on the severity and circumstances of the incident.
